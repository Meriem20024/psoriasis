# Data sources

- **Dataset:** GSE34248, "Gene expression profiling in psoriatic lesional and non-lesional skin [Set 1]"
- **Repository:** NCBI Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO)
- **Page:** https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE34248
- **Publication (PubMed ID):** 23308107
- **Platform:** GPL570 (Affymetrix Human Genome U133 Plus 2.0), 54,675 probes
- **Content:** 28 skin biopsies from 14 patients (one lesional and one non-lesional sample each)
- **Downloaded on:** 2026-11-01
